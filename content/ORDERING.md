## Allergy Notice

**Allergy Notice:**

KristaKookies are made in a home kitchen that handles common allergens, including wheat, dairy, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, and soy. I take great care in preparing each order, but I cannot guarantee that products are completely free from cross-contact.

If you have a severe allergy, please keep this in mind before placing your order. If you have any specific concerns, feel free to reach out. I'm always happy to chat and answer questions 💛
